Metro Surveys

Product Description
Metro is a channel wholesaler with a B2B model, focusing on HORECA (Hotel/Restaurant/Café) and TRADERS (small convenience stores, mom and pop stores). TCCC is in an agreement with Metro, mandated via the respective bottlers for each of the markets. 5 countries are operated by CCEP (France, Germany, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain), 12 from CCH (Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Italy, Moldova, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia) and 3 (Turkey, Kazakhstan and Pakistan) from CCI.

 

TCCC and Metro collaborate to establish a pay-for-performance system based on the implementation status of agreed-upon initiatives related to several strategic performances as follows.

 

For NARTD

Data - ensuring data about customers, volume, revenue is shared via an online tool of Metro named Customer Lens
Brand Penetration - each market agrees 2 programs for increase of brand penetration for TCCC brands excl. Coca-Cola Regular and respective objectives based on customer number
Pack Penetration - each market agrees 2 programs and objectives for specific pack penetration and respective objectives based on customer number
Occasion Activation - each market agrees 3 programs and objectives related to activation of Metro customers, activation of Metro warehouses and activations in HORECA online platform
Digital B2B E-Commerce - annual workshops to align on online ordering programs and execution on agreed plan
 

For Costa and Topo Chico:

Data - revenue is shared via an online tool of Metro named Customer Lens
Costa or Topo Chico specific performance
 

Metro Survey tool serves each Metro market to register the agreed initiatives (called Programs) and the planned revenue and further to collect the status of these initiatives on quarter base plus the profit on monthly base. When this data is collected, it is used to be calculated the payment to Metro.

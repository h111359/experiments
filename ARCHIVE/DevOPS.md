# GDATD DevOPS model

## Same team for development, technical operations, production management and services
- No separation between development and support capacity
- Everyone in the team is capable to do any of the activities
- Team members quickly jump in the other role in case of urgency

## Meaning or DevOPS model

Dev = Development – Building new features and enhancements according to roadmap/backlog
O = Operations – maintenance, technical procedures, deployment automation, CI/CD 
P = Production Management – monitoring, change management, security, reliability
S = Service – technical requests, incidents management, audit and support cases

---

## Benefits

- **Effectiveness** – the incidents are resolved quicker and easier as the developer usually has deeper understanding of how the system works.
- **Motivation** – the developer strives to provide high quality code so no incidents appear later.
- **End-to-End** – when support is made by the developers, their decisions after that reflect the knowledge gained while they have been on support.
- **Dynamic capacity allocation** – the effort can be rapidly shifted between support and development depending on the number of incidents.
- **Knowledge** – no need for a separate team to be trained and kept informed for any change. Dev team has this knowledge naturally. In addition, doing support speeds onboarding of new people in the team. Also the knowledge for the solutions is kept internally.
- **Be, feel and act as a technical owner** - Be enabled to take technical decisions. Be on the driving seat for the product roadmap, planning and change management, vendor management.

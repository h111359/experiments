## Description of the team

GDATD stands for Global Data and Analytics Technologies Delivery Team in Sofia, Bulgaria. Our vision is: 'We, the GDC Sofia D&A Team, exemplify excellence in Data and Analytics.' We adhere to principles of Empowerment, Tolerance, Inclusion, Continuous Improvement, and Agility.

We are a team of 24 people. The team includes team leaders, data engineers, data analysts, a web developer, and a scrum master. 18 of the team members are Data Engineers working primarily with Microsoft Azure cloud technologies. In addition there are 1 Scrum Master, 1 Web developer with React and Django and 3 Data Analysts with focus on Microsoft Power BI and data processing.

We translate business needs for data and analytics into solution architectures, analyze the data sources, create end-to-end Data and Analytics products, implement the products in production and act after that as technical owners of these product by execution technical support and service requests as well as maintenance while continue to provide further enhancements in cooperation with the business owners and stakeholders. 

Our tasks include:
- data clearance
- data validation
- integration of data solution components
- Extract-Load-Transform-Load processes
- creation of reports and dashboards
- web interface for data visualization or administration of the product
- Incidents management and troubleshooting

As a project management we use Scrum process, which we have customized and adapted for a data and analytics team specifics and we have developed own way of work routines. We are following a DevOps model which relies on performing from the team of both development and technical support activities which improves a lot of the quality and the efficiency of the product and the team in many aspects - speed, stability, cost, reliability and know-how. 

Our model is open for extension of the team with other team members or vendor consultants in case of need of boosting the capacity temporarely or permanently, still keepting the responsibility and knowlege internally. For the purpose we have created:
* Onboarding process appropriate for self-serve quick new members involvement
* Product documentation template for standardized and reach documentation
* Development and deployment standard approaches

## How we do it

## Welcoming
- GDATD Onboarding Page - single place for a newcommer to start from in self-paced tempo
- Team Connect Page - our enterprise visible web page
- Accesses - a series of defined access groups for efficient onboarding

## Onboarding
- *Help from a buddy*  - the newcommer is assigned an experienced team member for day-to-day questions and help
- Team OneNote - shared documentation and collaboration file with all important for the team information, trainings, materials, alighment, etc.
- NSR/CCL Onboarding - specific tracks for the different product to be taken after general onboarding is completed

## Growing
- Training Curriculum - collection of training materials and tracks for upskilling and learning
- Thrive, Datacamp, Udemy - access to external training resourses

## Delivering
- Practice - after onboarding is completed, participaction in the development and support processes of the products of the team give a lot of hands-on experience
- Support and Client Facing - besides the technical work, everyone from the team is expected to have domain knowledge and to participate in end user requirements gathering processes

## Seniority
- Design User Stories - be the one inventing the best solution provides broader perspective and much wholistic knowledge
- Features Ownership - transition from a solo contributor to a leadership activities with increased responsibilities
- Relationships and exposion - become a point of contact, expert for the domain, go-to person 

## Lead

- Processes, Strategy, Goals - tink end-to-end for a products, form roadmaps, ensure stability and security, product ownership

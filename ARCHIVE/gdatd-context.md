## 2. Operating Model (DevOps)
We embrace a unified DevOPS model: the same team executes <D>evelopment, <O>perations, <O>production management and <S>ervice work. This ensures faster feedback loops, higher code quality, and sustained product knowledge.

Same team for development, technical operations, production management and services:
- No separation between development and support capacity
- Everyone in the team is capable to do any of the activities
- Team members quickly jump in the other role in case of urgency

### DevOPS abbreviation - the pillars

Dev = Development – Building new features and enhancements according to roadmap/backlog
O = Operations – maintenance, technical procedures, deployment automation, CI/CD 
P = Production Management – monitoring, change management, security, reliability
S = Service – technical requests, incidents management, audit and support cases

### Benefits

- **Effectiveness** – the incidents are resolved quicker and easier as the developer usually has deeper understanding of how the system works.
- **Motivation** – the developer strives to provide high quality code so no incidents appear later.
- **End-to-End** – when support is made by the developers, their decisions after that reflect the knowledge gained while they have been on support.
- **Dynamic capacity allocation** – the effort can be rapidly shifted between support and development depending on the number of incidents.
- **Knowledge** – no need for a separate team to be trained and kept informed for any change. Dev team has this knowledge naturally. In addition, doing support speeds onboarding of new people in the team. Also the knowledge for the solutions is kept internally.
- **Be, feel and act as a technical owner** - Be enabled to take technical decisions. Be on the driving seat for the product roadmap, planning and change management, vendor management.

### NSR Platform – Scope Snapshot
- Standardized ingestion from diverse ERP systems
- Master data consolidation & data quality governance
- Concurrency improvements & orchestration modernization
- Data democratization & automation features
- The Net Sales Revenue (NSR) platform is a comprehensive system used by TCCC to collect both volume and revenue data from bottlers (System NSR), streamlining the consolidation of large volumes of master data for easier cross-country and cross-bottler reporting. NSR incorporates standardized raw data ingestion from various ERP systems, ensuring data completeness, accuracy, and simplified maintenance of master data. The platform aims to enhance efficiency and utility by addressing functional and technical challenges, such as improving concurrency, data democratization, and modernizing the orchestration engine.

### Metro Survey application (pay‑for‑performance program tracking)
- Programs & strategic initiatives status
- Planned vs actual revenue (EUR & local currency)
- Program activation metrics (brand, pack, occasion, digital)
- New buying customers & activated locations
- Actual & projected payment calculations

### RED Certification – Capability Summary
- Central data store + Power BI analytical layer
- The RED Certification process is a component of the RED (Right Execution Daily) process, ensuring that strategic business tools are adopted effectively to drive shopper satisfaction. 
- RED Certification tool is a web-based survey system for collection of RED performance data and providing reporting and analysis over it. By transitioning from manual Excel-based surveys to a web-based application, the project reduces costs and enhances functionality. 
- The centralized system will feature a user interface for assessments, a database for storing results, and Power BI integration for data analysis and is planned for go live in 2025. 

### CuDa (Customer Data) analytics solutions
- CuDa Carrefour is a data integration and reporting tool for analysis of retail POS and Shopper data from Carrefour across over 20 countries in Europe, LATAM, EME, and Africa. It involves collecting both monthly and weekly data, which is integrated into a Power BI reports which aims to provide additional insights from data available in the Carrefour
- The CuDa Metro product utilizes sell out revenue and buying customers data to monitor and drive business growth by incentivizing the sale of the appropriate SKUs to the targeted customer segments. The platform compiles monthly data from Metro across over 20 countries, involving bottlers CCEP, CCH, and CCI, and integrates it into a Power BI report featuring critical KPIs such as revenue and customer metrics comparing current and previous year performance. The data, segmented by customer type, packaging size, and SKU, is aggregated annually and monthly for detailed analysis. Our objective is to promote specific SKU sales tailored to each segment, enhancing strategic decision-making. 
- QSRP (Quick Service Restaurant Platform) offers valuable insights on PITA (Population, Incidence, Transactions, Amounts) tracking categorized by outlet location, daypart, menu type and size, brand, and country. This comprehensive PBI dashboard empowers teams to understand consumer behaviors and sales drivers (total trips, KO incidence, average units per trip and price per unit) currently in Burger King Belgium and Italy with a perspective to include other brands like Nordsee, O'Tacos and Quick in other European countries. In addition is available a dashboard focused on a new product launch by Frozen Brothers (Frozen Coke and Frozen Fanta) in 12 pilot outlets in BK Italy and Belgium which aims at understanding whether this event leads to traffic increase, cannibalization, incidence change. By visualizing key performance indicators, the dashboard supports strategic decision-making and collaborative planning for growth with the bottlers and QSRP. The project signifies a pivotal advancement in leveraging data to drive sales and increase market presence in the fast-evolving QSR landscape.
